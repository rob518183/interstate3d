**Interstate 3D** is a **modern revival** of the classic Interstate '76/'82 vehicular combat games, built with **cutting-edge technology** and **next-generation features**. This roadmap outlines our **strategic development plan** to create the **ultimate open-world vehicular combat experience**.

### **🎨 PHASE 2: ADVANCED RENDERING & VOXELS** 🔄 **READY TO START**

#### **🎯 OBJECTIVE**: Implement **cutting-edge graphics** and **voxel-based terrain**

#### **🏗️ MAJOR FEATURES**:

**🌟 ADVANCED RENDERING PIPELINE**
- 🔄 **Cascaded Shadow Maps**: High-quality directional shadows
- 🔄 **Global Illumination**: Voxel cone tracing for realistic lighting
- 🔄 **Screen-Space Effects**: SSAO, SSR, motion blur
- 🔄 **Post-Processing Stack**: Bloom, tone mapping, color grading
- 🔄 **Material System**: Advanced PBR with subsurface scattering

**🧊 VOXEL TERRAIN SYSTEM**
- 🔄 **Marching Cubes**: Smooth voxel terrain generation
- 🔄 **Dynamic Deformation**: Real-time terrain modification
- 🔄 **Procedural Generation**: Infinite terrain with biomes
- 🔄 **Voxel Optimization**: Chunk-based LOD system
- 🔄 **Destruction System**: Voxel-based environment destruction

**🚗 ENHANCED VEHICLE SYSTEM**
- 🔄 **Advanced Physics**: Suspension, tire model, aerodynamics
- 🔄 **Damage System**: Component-based vehicle damage
- 🔄 **Customization**: Visual and performance upgrades
- 🔄 **Weapon Systems**: Multiple weapon types with ballistics
- 🔄 **Particle Effects**: Exhaust, smoke, fire, impacts

**🌍 ENVIRONMENT SYSTEM**

- 🔄 **Vegetation System**: Procedural flora placement
